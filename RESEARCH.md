# Research contract and next physical questions

The target is high-realism human speech generated by an articulated physical
body, with streamed gestures, diphthongs, speech habits, configurable anatomy,
and at least 10× realtime rendering. This target is still active. "Perfect"
will not be inferred from tests that establish only numerical correctness.

## Acceptance evidence

- **Physics:** dimensional consistency, passivity for undriven frozen geometry,
  resonance/antiresonance checks against analytical cases and higher-fidelity
  models, and bounded behavior during closures and body movement.
- **Engine:** one continuous acoustic/body state under incoming nudges; finite,
  unclipped output; no rendering allocation; host buffer size independence;
  bounded queues with explicit rejection rather than silent cue loss.
- **Efficiency:** every measured run of a representative matrix at least 10×
  realtime. Prepare, steady render, diagnostic overhead, and total wall time
  are separate. A slower model triggers a representation investigation.
- **Human speech:** matched-word/vowel recordings and blinded listening. Check
  consonant identity, vowel transitions, word intelligibility, breath onset,
  prosody, sibilants, and speaker consistency separately. No ASR is being used
  as a surrogate for the listening criterion.
- **Body matching:** preserve the distinction between measured anatomy,
  acoustically inferred parameters, and free nuisance parameters. Three
  formants do not uniquely identify a tongue or vocal tract.

## Representation finding

The supplied detailed surface implementation advances 4,988 directed delayed
paths at 192 kHz for a 563-piece body. The existing modal implementation advances
roughly 20 acoustic modes at 48 kHz with warm-started geometry solves at 1 kHz.
The fresh baseline is about 0.179× versus 18.2× realtime on the same vowel/glide
probe. Even the surface implementation's one-piece-per-ring tube is only 2×.

This points to **acoustic states that couple to the sources and radiators** as
the useful representation, not every path between wall pieces. It does not
prove that the current tree-modal anatomy is a sufficient replacement.

The [frozen surface port operator](surface/FROZEN_PORTS.md) now eliminates
the detailed model's delay histories analytically at each frequency and solves
for its junction pressures. It retains every frozen path/filter and matches
six time-domain probes to at worst 3.77e-6 relative complex error over the
tested 48 Hz–12 kHz points. The full body has 563 pressure unknowns versus
319,232 allocated delay samples, but the operator is frequency-dependent:
this is a verified reduction reference, not yet a small dynamic renderer.

The [passive dynamic reduction](surface/PASSIVE_REDUCTION.md) now expresses the
same frozen network in 20,220 energy states, then projects anatomical blocks
to 501 states. All 30 port transfers have been checked at 2,401 frequencies.
A native coupled-fold probe reaches 19.68–19.97×, while the simultaneous
five-source/six-output path still fails the speed gate at 8.73–8.80×. Its offline
preparation and frozen-state limitation prevent using it as the moving engine.
Further reduction must preserve the weak cavity couplings and the energy
metric; blind modal pruning is not an acceptable shortcut.

A reduction must preserve the port responses at glottis, lips, nose, constriction
jets, and moving sublingual openings. Frozen-shape frequency response, phase,
loss and resonant residues should agree before moving-body speech is compared.
For moving geometry, passivity of a frozen mesh is not enough: account for work
by the articulators and for energy carried through coordinate changes. Merely
reducing ring count, skipping the floor, or lowering the sample rate will not
be labelled a successful reduction without these checks.

## Anatomical questions exposed by the existing experiments

1. The straight tongue blade cannot form a laminal alveolar groove while its
   tip stays down. Adding the floor exposed this error: the apical /s/ opens an
   unwanted sublingual pocket. A flexible blade must constrain both the airway
   above it and the tissue/floor space below it, while respecting reach and
   continuous motion. Do not fix this with an /s/-specific equalizer.
2. The /r/ gesture substitutes excessive lip narrowing for tongue-root and
   palatal constrictions. The prior area-sensitivity study predicted a wider
   lip opening and a pharyngeal constriction; body degrees of freedom must
   support this before adjusting the acoustic target.
3. The compact modal engine lacks the floor and explicit transverse states.
   Preserve these responses in its successor instead of declaring the old
   model equivalent because it passes the speed gate.
4. The two-mass fold aperture law had a hard minimum that creates corners when
   the flow bottleneck switches masses. Shared rounded contact removes this
   specific numerical source of excess harmonics. Its physical scales remain
   model parameters, not an individually measured larynx.
5. Coupling/damping and low-formant estimates still need source-aware analysis.
   The enhanced field helps locate transitions; ordinary spectral analysis and
   known simulated states must check features before changing anatomy.

6. The enhanced-field horizontal comb follows the control clock. Advancing
   physiological forces at audio rate removes most quiet-section components,
   but moving-geometry updates still introduce strong transition-region lines.
   [The controlled investigation](reports/CONTROL_CLOCK.md) makes continuous
   state transport a measured audio-quality requirement, not just an energy
   accounting concern.

Useful primary references:

- [Owens, Wright and Ramsey, 1989](https://www.isca-archive.org/eurospeech_1989/owens89b_eurospeech.html): articulators, lossy tube and two-mass folds in one model.
- [Toda and Honda, 2002](https://www.isca-archive.org/icslp_2002/toda02_icslp.html): sibilant differences include constriction length/width, sublingual cavity and lips.
- [1-D/3-D glottal flow comparison, 2022](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9629867/): comparison of 1-D and 3-D glottal flow under asymmetric fold conditions. Agreement in selected outputs does not establish equivalence for every geometry.

transport remains required for finer partitions and other contact geometries.

## Moving-energy reference

[Geometry transport investigation](reports/GEOMETRY_TRANSPORT.md) isolates the
remaining clock lines in state transfer. Merely choosing the cleaner pressure/
flow mapping does not establish correct work accounting. An exact uniform-area
reference proves that the default lower-energy mapping loses 75% of acoustic
energy during a fourfold area change, even at audio-rate subdivision. A new
2×2 propagator reproduces continuous canonical metric transport in that
restricted case and supplies a required limit for the nonuniform moving engine.
The default renderer has not been replaced by this reference kernel.
