## Implemented

- Strict, transactional SI-unit body configurations for geometry, fold
  mechanics, breathing, pitch habits and articulator speeds. Both CLIs accept
  `--body`; the modal CLI also accepts a validated `--rate`.
- A direct incremental nudge interface, independent of text planning, with
  overlapping diphthong/geometry/physiology targets, bounded pending cues,
  explicit late-cue rejection, and persistent body and acoustic state.
- A 48 kHz float32 stereo pipe CLI (`ducklips_stream`). The example feeds
  additional cues after rendering has begun. It rendered 3.3 s in about
  0.183 s (18.0×), excluding prepare and pipe I/O.
- Pre-limiter nonfinite/clipped sample counters. Numerical failure is no
  longer concealed by the existing finite/clamped output conversion in the
  benchmark or stream CLI.
- Explicit dictionary files now override built-ins. Previously the NATO file
  installed only three missing entries and silently left built-in stress or
  phonemes in place. It now installs 29 definitions, including aliases, with
  a regression check on Papa and Quebec.
- Shared rounded fold aperture geometry. The compact engine previously used
  hard contact and a hard minimum between lower and upper openings, while
  the surface engine used rounded contact. The physical model parameters now
  default to contact softness 0.03 mm² and medial roundness 0.25 mm². A sharp
  control body remains available.

## Source-geometry experiment

On a sustained /a/, compare the **glottal-flow derivative** with identical body,
seed and cues, changing only the contact/minimum geometry. Analyze 0.9–1.7 s,
Welch 8192-sample segments, channel 0, relative to 100–1000 Hz source power:

| Band | Rounded minus sharp |
|---|---:|
| 1–4 kHz | −0.40 dB |
| 4–8 kHz | −0.96 dB |
| 8–16 kHz | −3.58 dB |
| 16–22 kHz | −6.83 dB |

This local result is consistent with removing a corner in the aperture's
trajectory: high harmonics change more than the speech midband. It does not
establish the correct individual fold shape or prove perceptual improvement.
There is no output EQ in the comparison. Reproduce with
`python tools/source_comparison.py`; values and waveform hashes are in
[source_geometry.json](source_geometry.json).

## Current speed gate

Apple M4, macOS 26.5 arm64, Apple clang 21, CMake Release, one render thread,
48 kHz stereo, 64 oral and 24 nasal sections, cutoff 10 kHz, 1 ms control.
The NATO alphabet is rendered three times per case. Every run passes the
**render-only** 10× floor and has zero pre-limiter nonfinite/clipped samples.

| Case | Slowest render | Median render | Slowest total process incl. prepare/I/O |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| baritone | 12.58× | 12.58× | 10.51× |
| alto | 13.89× | 14.06× | 11.53× |
| child | 18.54× | 18.72× | 15.62× |
| gravel | 12.97× | 13.03× | 10.79× |
| whisper | 15.27× | 15.35× | 12.23× |
| custom body | 12.97× | 12.98× | 10.88× |
| double-speed baritone | 12.44× | 12.45× | **9.94×** |

Cold-process throughput is not universally above 10×. Preparation includes a
vowel-posture inversion and fold calibration, paid once per configured body;
keep a body alive across streamed speech instead of rebuilding it per word.
The explicit render-only criterion does not hide this startup cost. Times
include planned silence and pauses. The full enhanced-field computation took
about 15–19 s per excerpt and is strictly offline diagnostics.

Full runs, commands, binary hash, platform and output audits:
[performance.json](performance.json). An enhanced-field job overlapped part of
this benchmark on another CPU; no claim of an otherwise idle machine is made.

The baseline representation comparison used identical phonemes
`AA1:2 IY1:2 UW1:2 | S IY1 SH AO1 R`:

| Representation | Audio | Compute | Speed |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Original compact modal | 7.88 s | 0.433 s | 18.2× |
| Full surface (563 pieces, 4988 directed paths) | 7.88 s | 44.10 s | **0.179×** |

The single-ring surface tube on a separate 2 s /a/ probe achieved only 2×.
A fresh full-surface custom-body Alfa render also built and ran, but required
7.90 s for 1.31 s audio (0.166×); this is an integration check, not acceptance
of its speed. The modal engine does not yet preserve the floor/transverse
physics of the surface engine, so it is not a completed replacement.

## Validation and remaining work

The subsequent [frozen surface operator investigation](../surface/FROZEN_PORTS.md)
now supplies an equivalent frequency-domain pressure/flow reference for
reducing the detailed model. Six probes match complex responses to at worst
3.77e-6 relative error, retaining the transverse and floor paths. The full
body still renders its instrumented impulse at only 0.20×; the new operator is
an offline verification tool, not a replacement speech engine. Its release and
ASan/UBSan runs each pass all three registered test suites. Full measurements
are in [frozen_surface_ports.json](frozen_surface_ports.json).

- Release CTest: both numerical-core and new streaming/configuration suites
  pass. The latter checks dictionary precedence, invalid input, transactional
  failure, overflow, late cues, exact host-buffer independence (17-frame chunks
  versus a full buffer), finite/nonzero output, and no render allocations.
- AddressSanitizer + UndefinedBehaviorSanitizer: both suites pass.
- Both surface and compact executables build; both render a custom body and
  the explicit NATO dictionary.
- Python reference preparation/hash checks and the enhanced-field/paired-view
  workflow run successfully.
- `--rate 2junk` is rejected; `git diff --check` passes.
- The optional GUI.Forms adapter was not built. No new native audio-device
  test or human listening test has been performed in this run.

The goal remains active. High human realism, individual-speaker matching,
flexible tongue blade/root gestures, and detailed-physics rendering at 10×
are **not complete**. [RESEARCH.md](../RESEARCH.md) retains these as acceptance
requirements rather than redefining them around the fast model's limitations.

## Passive dynamic reduction follow-up

The [energy-coordinate investigation](../surface/PASSIVE_REDUCTION.md) now
reproduces the frozen operator dynamically, preserves anatomical weak
connections during projection, and runs a 501-state native prototype. The
2,401-frequency/all-30-transfer check reports a worst 0.000147 dB discrepancy
above its declared per-transfer level threshold. Native impulse verification
agrees with the reduced equations within 2.49e-6 on its declared contour.

Five repeated coupled-fold probes reach 19.68–19.97× at 192 kHz. Five repeated
simultaneous-source/all-output probes reach only 8.73–8.80×, so the benchmark
**correctly fails** the 10× gate. The renderer is not accepted, and no moving
speech implementation has been replaced. Failed global reductions, the lateral
source error exposed by broader validation, and unsuccessful loop blocking are
documented rather than hidden. Full receipts and hashes are in
[passive_surface_reduction.json](passive_surface_reduction.json).

Release and ASan/UBSan each pass four CTest suites; three Python component
contracts pass. These establish numerical/software behavior, not human realism.
